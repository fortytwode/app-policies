Little Airplanes support

Contact us

Little Airplanes is published by Rocketship HQ OÜ. For help with the app, membership, or privacy, email hi@rocketshiphq.com. Tell us that you use Little Airplanes and describe the problem. Please do not send your child's name or other sensitive information in your first message.

Manage or cancel your membership

Little Airplanes offers an annual subscription through Apple. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Little Airplanes, then use the available manage or cancel option. Apple's cancellation instructions have the current steps. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Restore a purchase

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. In Little Airplanes, open a locked world from the world shelf to show the parent plan screen. Tap Restore Purchases and complete the parent gate. If the purchase still does not appear, check Apple's restore-purchases guidance and email us with the app name, device model, and a description of what happened. Please do not send payment-card details or a child's profile answers.

Optional child profile

No account or password is needed for the optional profile. The answers stay on your device. To remove them, open For parents → Welcome & how to play, go to the profile screen, and tap Delete these answers. You can continue using the app without filling in the questions. See the Little Airplanes privacy policy for the separate subscription-service data flow.
