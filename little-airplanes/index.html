Privacy Policy for Little Airplanes

Little Airplanes is an airplane-play app for young children and their families, published by Rocketship HQ OÜ in Estonia. A parent or guardian should make decisions about the optional profile, privacy choices, and membership.

Which versions this policy covers

This policy describes earlier versions of Little Airplanes and versions that include Privacy choices. The optional analytics and crash-report sections apply only to versions with Privacy choices. Earlier RevenueCat-only versions do not include Firebase Analytics, Meta App Events or Crashlytics collection. A new policy does not add those features to an older installed version.

Family information stays on this device

Optional onboarding can include a child's name, age range, and a parent's choices about interests or support. Those answers are stored on this device to suggest a world. Family profile information and photos are stored locally; they are not sent to the analytics, crash-reporting, subscription, or paywall services described below. You can skip the questions or use Delete these answers on the profile screen. Preferences, play state, and the last known membership status are also stored on this device. Deleting local answers removes them from this app's local storage; it does not remove Apple purchase records or provider records already created for a purchase.

Optional family photos and wooden characters

A parent can choose up to four wooden characters or assign one photo to each family member using Apple's photo picker or the camera. Little Airplanes receives only the image you choose. Its saved copies, crop, outfit choices, and family-member names stay on this device; they are not uploaded or shared with Apple, RevenueCat, or another third party. The original Photos library items are not changed, and saved app copies are excluded from device backups. In the parent guide's Your family section, you can edit or remove family members or switch them to wooden characters. Deleting the app removes the locally saved family profile; it does not remove Apple or RevenueCat purchase records.

Optional usage analytics

In versions with Privacy choices, a parent can turn on Share usage analytics in Privacy choices after passing the parent gate. It starts off. When enabled, Google Firebase Analytics receives app and device identifiers and a limited set of app-use events, such as onboarding steps, worlds opened, and play actions. Firebase may also collect automatically logged app engagement and in-app purchase metadata. This collection continues during play while the choice is on. These events do not include a child's name, profile answers, or family photos.

Meta receives only parent-screen funnel events and verified trial or direct-purchase acquisition events to measure campaign outcomes; it does not receive gameplay events. The app disables Meta automatic event logging, automatic event suggestions and advertising-ID collection. Automatic advanced matching is also disabled in the Meta app configuration. Meta may still process a persistent anonymous identifier and technical device or network metadata needed for requests and measurement. Turning off the advertising identifier does not mean that no identifiers or technical data are processed.

After a parent enables usage analytics, Meta may also use Apple’s SKAdNetwork for aggregate ad-install and conversion attribution. This does not enable advertising-ID collection or send child profile fields. Turning analytics off stops new app-authored events, but Meta has no supported way to tear down its SKAdNetwork processing: earlier or in-flight Apple conversion updates may complete, and the initialized SDK may reevaluate previously cached conversion state when the app returns to the foreground.

Turning this choice off stops further app-authored analytics events and disables and resets Firebase Analytics collection. Events already sent or in flight cannot be recalled. Meta has no SDK teardown; earlier consented requests may already be queued or in flight. These optional analytics choices do not control the services needed to show a parent subscription screen or verify a purchase.

Optional crash reports

A parent can separately turn on Share crash reports after passing the parent gate. It starts off. Automatic Crashlytics uploads remain off. Technical crash reports may be queued on this device, including during play, and are sent only on a later app launch if crash-report consent was on previously and remains on now. First-time opt-in deletes reports queued before consent. Turning off consent deletes pending reports. A report already sent or in flight cannot be recalled. Reports are not supplemented with child profiles or photos.

Membership, paywalls, and purchases

Apple processes the in-app purchase. RevenueCat handles subscription offerings, purchase status, membership checks, and restores; it is the app's only purchase and entitlement authority. RevenueCat creates an anonymous app user identifier and processes purchase or receipt information and technical metadata needed for those services. The app does not create a named RevenueCat account or send child profile information to it.

Superwall may deliver a remote subscription screen only after a parent reaches a subscription offer during onboarding or from a locked world and passes the parent gate. The app turns off Superwall event tracking and does not send it purchase events or child profile information. Superwall still processes an anonymous app or vendor identifier, device/app information, and ordinary request metadata needed to deliver that screen. RevenueCat, not Superwall, determines membership access.

Apple handles payment. Little Airplanes does not ask for payment-card details in the app. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. Deleting Little Airplanes does not cancel a subscription.

Children's privacy and choices

The optional profile and photos remain in local app storage. We do not include child names, profile answers, or photos in the events or reports described in this policy. Purchase, restore, external links, and remote subscription screens are placed behind a parent gate. Privacy choices do not change membership or access to worlds. A parent can change either choice from Privacy choices or For parents.

Your questions and provider information

You can leave the optional profile blank or delete its answers in the app. For privacy questions, or to ask about subscription data or request its deletion, email hi@rocketshiphq.com. We will review requests concerning information handled by RevenueCat for this app. Please do not include a child's name or other sensitive information in an initial support message. Removing local answers does not remove Apple's subscription records or RevenueCat purchase records. You can review Firebase privacy information, Meta's privacy policy, Superwall's privacy policy, RevenueCat's privacy policy, and Apple's privacy information.

We will update this policy when Little Airplanes' data practices change. The current published policy should be available from the app's Privacy Policy link and its App Store page.
