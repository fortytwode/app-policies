Privacy Policy for Little Airplanes

Little Airplanes is an airplane-play app for young children and their families, published by Rocketship HQ OÜ in Estonia. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. A parent or guardian should make decisions about the optional profile and membership.

Your child's profile stays on the device

The optional onboarding questions can include a child's name, age range, and a parent's choices about interests or support. Little Airplanes saves those answers on the device to suggest a world to try. The app does not send the child's name or those answers to our subscription service. You can skip the questions or use Delete these answers on the profile screen. The app also saves local preferences, play state, and the last known membership status on the device.

Membership and purchases

Little Airplanes uses Apple In-App Purchase for its annual membership and RevenueCat to show the available subscription, check membership access, process purchase status, and restore purchases. RevenueCat creates an anonymous app user identifier for this app. RevenueCat receives purchase history and transaction information, such as an Apple purchase receipt, so it can verify and maintain membership. It may also process technical information needed to provide that service. We do not send the child's onboarding name or answers to RevenueCat, and the app does not create a named account with RevenueCat.

Apple handles the purchase transaction. Little Airplanes does not ask for payment-card details in the app. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings. Deleting Little Airplanes does not cancel a subscription.

Advertising and activity

The current Little Airplanes app has no third-party advertising SDK, does not show ads, and does not use advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking for advertising. It does not send the child's play choices or onboarding answers to an analytics service. RevenueCat can provide subscription and purchase reports from the purchase information described above.

Children's privacy

Little Airplanes is designed for children with a parent or guardian. The optional profile is kept on the device. Membership checks involve RevenueCat as described above, and purchase processing involves Apple. We do not use the child's profile for advertising. Purchase, restore, and links out of the app are placed behind a parent gate.

Your choices and questions

You can leave the optional profile blank or delete its answers in the app. If you want to ask about subscription data or request its deletion, email us at hi@rocketshiphq.com. Please do not include a child's name or other sensitive information in an initial support message. We will review requests concerning information handled by RevenueCat for this app. Removing locally saved answers does not remove Apple's subscription records or RevenueCat purchase records. You can also review RevenueCat's privacy information and Apple's privacy information.

We will update this policy when Little Airplanes' data practices change. The current version should always be available from the app's Privacy Policy link and its App Store page.
