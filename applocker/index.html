AppLocker Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

AppLocker helps you limit access to distracting apps during a schedule you choose. Blocking and your app selections work on your device. Optional Pro subscriptions use online purchase and paywall services.

Information stored on your device

AppLocker stores the package names of apps you choose to keep available, your schedules and extra focus windows, temporary pause state, and a salted verifier for your local PIN. The PIN is not stored in plain text. Creating a PIN is required before changing settings; it also protects pause and resume controls.

These settings stay in private local storage and are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. AppLocker does not send app selections, schedules, PINs or accessibility events to RevenueCat or Superwall.

Accessibility Service

With your permission, AppLocker uses Android's Accessibility Service to observe foreground app/package changes and apply your schedule. When an app outside your allowed selection opens during an active focus window, AppLocker returns you to Home and attempts to show an explanatory blocking screen. Android may prevent that screen from appearing.

The service does not retrieve window content, read text or passwords, capture screens, record audio, or transmit accessibility data. You can disable it at any time in Android Settings.

App visibility and notifications

AppLocker queries launchable apps for the app picker, and identifies Home, Settings and dialer apps to keep essential controls accessible. This information stays on your device. The app does not request permission to query every installed package. With notification permission, its foreground service displays Focus Mode status and PIN-protected pause and resume controls.

Subscriptions, analytics and service providers

Google Play processes subscription payments. RevenueCat validates purchases and restores and provides Pro subscription status. Superwall displays subscription offers and measures the purchase experience. These services process purchase history, subscription and transaction identifiers, anonymous app and device identifiers, device and app information, and paywall interactions and technical events such as loads, errors and purchase results. Superwall may use install-referrer information and derive an approximate region or city from your network address. Network connections also expose your IP address to the receiving service.

We use this information for app functionality and analytics, including improving subscription screens. AppLocker has no sign-in account, and we do not send your name or email to these services. We do not receive your payment card details, use advertising networks or sell your personal information. Service data is transmitted using encrypted connections.

See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Superwall's privacy policy. Providers may process data outside your country and retain it as needed to operate subscriptions, resolve purchase issues, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

Your control and data deletion

Uninstall AppLocker or use Android Settings → Apps → AppLocker → Storage → Clear data to remove local settings and the PIN. Revoking Accessibility access stops enforcement. The PIN does not prevent these Android controls.

Uninstalling or clearing data does not cancel your Google Play subscription or delete purchase-service records. Manage subscriptions in Google Play. To request access to or deletion of subscription-service data, email the contact below and include the subscription support ID shown in the app if available. We may retain records needed for active purchases or legal requirements.
