Notetaker AI Privacy Policy

Effective November 28, 2026.

Rocketship HQ OU provides Notetaker AI for iPhone and Apple Watch. You can use the iPhone app without a Notetaker account. TestFlight builds may differ in which of the services below they include.

Your recordings and notes

When you choose to record or import a file, the app keeps the audio, transcripts, summaries, and other note information in app storage on your device. For a supported video file the app extracts and saves its audio track. Apple Speech and Apple Intelligence process notes on the device. The app does not send recordings or notes to Rocketship HQ OU servers. Apple Speech may download language assets.

The app requests microphone access when you choose to record. Optional live transcription may also request speech recognition access. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings.

On supported builds, you can ask the app to display Apple Calendar events beside your recordings. Notetaker reads the events on your device when you allow calendar access. It does not send them to us. You can change access in iOS Settings.

On supported builds, you can record on a paired Apple Watch and transfer the recording to Notetaker on your iPhone. The recording waits on the watch until the phone receives it. This feature does not upload audio to a Notetaker server.

If you enable Find notes in Spotlight, the app indexes saved note titles on your iPhone. Turn the setting off in Notetaker to remove those titles from Spotlight.

Sharing and backups

Notes leave the app when you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet. The recipient or service you select handles that copy. Your iOS backup settings may also include app data. Delete the original recording and related note using the note menu. Manage backups separately in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions

Apple handles payments for optional Notetaker AI Pro subscriptions. The app checks subscription access using StoreKit. Rocketship HQ OU does not receive your payment card details.

RevenueCat and Superwall receive subscription and transaction information, such as the product, purchase, and entitlement status, for subscription features and reporting. The app uses anonymous provider identifiers for this reporting.

Optional ad measurement

The app asks for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, we use Meta’s SDK to measure which ads led to installs and other activity. Meta can receive events such as app launches and purchases, device identifiers, and technical information, including crash information. Meta uses this information for ad measurement, analytics, and SDK operation. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings at any time.

Questions and support

Email hi@rocketshiphq.com or visit Notetaker AI Support. Please leave recordings and transcripts out of support emails unless you choose to share them.
