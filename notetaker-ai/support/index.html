Notetaker AI Support

Email hi@rocketshiphq.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were trying to do. Please leave private audio and note contents out of your email.

Microphone access

Open Settings on your iPhone, select Notetaker AI, and enable Microphone. Then open the app and tap New recording.

Transcription and AI availability

Apple Speech and Apple Intelligence support depends on your iPhone, operating system, language, and locale. A language download may be needed. You can still play and share saved recordings when transcription is unavailable.

Managing Notetaker AI Pro

Go to Settings on your iPhone, tap your Apple Account, and select Subscriptions. You can also manage an active plan in the app’s Settings. Use Restore Purchases on the offer screen to recover a subscription bought with the same Apple Account.

Removing notes and recordings

Open the note’s three-dot menu and choose Delete note. Doing this removes the associated local audio.
