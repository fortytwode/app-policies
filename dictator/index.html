Dictator Privacy Policy

Dictator is a voice dictation keyboard for iPhone, provided by Rocketship HQ OÜ. Dictator does not require an account and does not display third-party advertisements. This policy explains the information the app uses and when it leaves your device.

Microphone and dictation

When you start a dictation session, Dictator keeps the microphone available while the session is active so its keyboard can begin dictation while you are using another app. iOS shows its microphone indicator during this time. Between dictations, the app keeps a short audio pre-roll of up to about 0.35 seconds in memory to avoid cutting off the first syllable. Audio buffers are sent to the selected speech recognizer only while a dictation is in progress. Dictator does not write microphone audio to disk.

With Apple speech recognition, speech is processed by Apple's on-device speech services. You may optionally choose a Whisper speech model; that model is downloaded from Hugging Face/Argmax to your device and speech is then processed on the device. The app does not send recorded audio to Rocketship HQ or Anthropic.

Optional Anthropic cleanup

AI cleanup can use Apple Intelligence on device. Claude cleanup is optional and requires both an Anthropic API key and a separate permission in Dictator Settings. Unless you enable Allow sending dictation to Anthropic, Claude is not used, including when an older saved setting selects Claude.

When that permission is enabled and Claude cleanup is selected, Dictator sends Anthropic the dictated transcript, up to 500 characters of surrounding text from the keyboard field (up to 250 characters before and after the cursor), and relevant spelling hints. Spelling hints can include your saved dictionary terms and your name. The selected text is not included in the cleanup prompt. Audio is not sent. Anthropic processes the request under its own terms and privacy practices. Turning permission off prevents future Claude requests; it cannot recall information already sent.

You can remove your Anthropic API key in Dictator Settings. Dictator stores the key in the iOS Keychain. If Claude is unavailable, cleanup falls back to Apple Intelligence when available, then basic on-device cleanup.

Free dictation and Dictator Pro

Dictator is free to download and includes up to 1,000 dictated words per week on this iPhone. The word counter is stored on the device and resets every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Dictator Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription for unlimited voice dictation on iPhone. It costs $9.99 USD per month, has no free trial, and does not include a Mac entitlement. Apple processes subscription payments and manages renewals. Dictator does not receive payment card details. You can restore a purchase in Dictator or manage and cancel the subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Subscriptions are handled by Apple’s App Store. Dictator does not receive full payment information. Build 6 does not include RevenueCat or Superwall and does not use an analytics SDK.

Information stored on your iPhone

Dictator stores settings, your optional name, dictionary entries, text snippets, and keyboard communication files in the app's shared iOS App Group so the app and keyboard can work together. Dictation history is stored in the app's private local database. You choose whether history is kept forever, for 30 days, for 7 days, or not saved. You can delete individual history items or clear all history from the History screen. Removing the app removes its local app data. Model downloads can be deleted in Settings.

These local files and the weekly free-word counter are not sent to Rocketship HQ. Dictionary spelling hints and the name may be included in the Claude request only when Claude content permission is enabled, as described above. Dictator does not create an account.

Versions with RevenueCat and Superwall enabled

This section applies to versions that include RevenueCat and Superwall, not build 6. These services help validate purchases, restore subscriptions, unlock Dictator Pro, display subscription offers, and measure how those offers work.

RevenueCat receives App Store purchase and subscription history under an automatically generated app user identifier. Dictator shares that identifier with Superwall so subscription status and paywall activity can be associated with the same app installation. No login, name, email address, or other contact attributes are supplied to either service.

Superwall receives paywall interactions and app-session events, a vendor device identifier, and technical information such as app and operating-system versions, device model, language, locale, and time zone. Its service can also derive an approximate region from the network IP address. Purchase and usage information is used for app functionality and analytics. Build 7 uses RevenueCat and Superwall for these purposes. A version that enables RevenueCat's Meta integration also uses RevenueCat to report subscription events for advertising measurement, as described below. Superwall is not used for ad attribution.

Dictator does not send microphone audio, dictated transcripts, surrounding keyboard text, dictionary entries, text snippets, your saved name, or the weekly word counter to RevenueCat or Superwall. The separate optional Anthropic cleanup described above is unchanged.

More information: RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Superwall Privacy Policy.

Versions with Firebase and Meta measurement enabled

This section applies to versions that include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Meta App Events. Build 7 and earlier do not include these SDKs. They run in the companion app, not the keyboard extension.

Google Analytics for Firebase records app use, including onboarding steps, permission outcomes, dictation starts and outcomes, processing duration, speech-engine choice, free-allowance limits, and subscription-screen interactions. Reports include technical information such as app and operating-system versions, device model, an app-instance identifier, and approximate region. The app gives RevenueCat that Firebase app-instance identifier so RevenueCat can report subscription purchases, renewals and other subscription events to Google Analytics in versions where that integration is enabled. Firebase Crashlytics collects crash stack traces and technical diagnostics so we can investigate failures. Analytics events may also appear as breadcrumbs in crash reports.

App events contain counts, timings and predefined status categories. They do not contain microphone audio, dictated text, surrounding keyboard text, dictionary entries, snippets, your saved name, or API keys. The app does not attach free-text dictation errors or a personal user profile to crash reports. Dictator disables Google ad personalization in the app.

Meta App Events measures app activation, onboarding, feature and subscription-screen activity, and verified purchases to measure promotion of Dictator. The app gives RevenueCat a Meta anonymous app identifier and device information. In versions where the RevenueCat–Meta integration is enabled, RevenueCat also reports subscription events and matching information, such as its app user identifier, device identifiers and IP address, to Meta for advertising measurement. Dictator asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before using the advertising identifier or tracking across other companies' apps and websites. Allowing tracking is optional and does not unlock or restrict any feature. The advertising identifier is available only when iOS reports that you have authorized tracking. You can change that permission in iPhone Settings. Declining it does not stop ordinary app analytics.

More information: Firebase Privacy and Security and Meta Privacy Policy. For requests concerning analytics or diagnostics data, contact us at the address below.
