ScreenBreak Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Your selected apps, focus settings and intervention history stay on this device. Subscription services receive purchase and paywall information to provide Pro and help us improve its purchase experience.

Screen Time reports

With your permission, Apple's Screen Time APIs display your device activity inside a protected report extension. ScreenBreak does not copy the report's activity history or resolved app names into its main app database or send them to RevenueCat or Superwall.

Settings and interventions

The app stores opaque tokens for the apps, categories and websites you select, focus schedules, daily limits, preferences and shield interaction records locally. Its extensions share the settings they need through an App Group. Interaction records older than your retention period (90 days by default) are removed when you open the app. These records and selections are not sent to our subscription providers.

Subscriptions and paywalls

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat validates purchases and restores, maintains subscription status and enables Pro. Superwall displays subscription offers and measures the purchase experience. These services process purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, subscription status, anonymous app and device identifiers, device and app information, and paywall interactions and technical events such as loads, errors and purchase results. Superwall may derive an approximate region or city from your network address. Network connections also expose your IP address to the service receiving the request.

We use this information for app functionality and analytics, including understanding which subscription screens work. No ScreenBreak account is required, and we do not provide these services with your name, email, selected apps, Screen Time history or intervention log. We do not receive your payment card details. We do not use advertising networks or sell your personal information.

For details about our providers, see RevenueCat's privacy policy and Superwall's privacy policy. Providers may process data in countries other than yours and retain information as needed to operate subscriptions, resolve purchase issues, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and use Apple's local notification service.

Export, deletion and subscription control

You may export ScreenBreak's intervention log to another app. That recipient's privacy practices apply to the copy you share. Settings lets you delete local ScreenBreak data and clear its restrictions. During strict focus, deletion becomes available after the session ends or after you revoke Screen Time permission in iOS Settings. Apple's Screen Time history is managed by iOS.

Deleting local data or uninstalling ScreenBreak does not delete subscription records held by Apple or our providers, and does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. For a request to access or delete subscription-service data, contact us using the address below and include the subscription support ID shown in the app if available. We may need to retain records required for an active purchase or by law.
