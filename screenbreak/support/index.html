ScreenBreak Support

For help with ScreenBreak, email shamanth@rocketshiphq.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and a description of what happened. For schedule problems, include your time zone and the time the issue occurred.

Screen Time access

Focus sessions, app limits and activity reports require Screen Time permission. If access has been revoked, return to ScreenBreak and follow its permission prompt.

Focus sessions

A flexible session can end early in ScreenBreak. A strict session keeps its in-app End control unavailable until the timer finishes. You can still revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases on the Pro screen to check purchases associated with your Apple Account. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Deleting ScreenBreak or its local data does not cancel a subscription.

Local data

ScreenBreak Settings offers export and deletion of the app's own interaction records. During strict focus, deletion becomes available after the session ends or Screen Time access is revoked. Apple's activity report is not included in the export.
